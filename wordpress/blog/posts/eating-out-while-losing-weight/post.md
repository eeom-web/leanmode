Losing weight doesn't mean you have to stop going out to eat. Restaurants, birthdays and dinners with friends are part of life. With a few simple strategies, you can enjoy them and still make progress.

## Before you go

### 1. Check the menu ahead

Most restaurants have their menu online. Choose your meal at home, when you're not hungry and not tempted by the smells and the table next to you.

### 2. Don't arrive starving

Skipping meals to "save" calories often backfires. Eat normally during the day and have a small, filling snack like yogurt or fruit before you go.

### 3. Decide what matters most to you

Do you love dessert? Then skip the bread and starter. Want a glass of wine? Choose a lighter main dish. Pick one or two treats instead of all of them.

## At the table

### 4. Start with water

Order a large glass of water as soon as you sit down and drink it throughout the meal.

### 5. Handle the bread basket

If you know you'll eat it all, ask the server not to bring it, or take one piece and send the basket back.

### 6. Start with a salad or soup

A side salad (with dressing on the side) or a broth-based soup takes the edge off your hunger.

### 7. Use the plate method

Look for a dish with protein and vegetables, and add carbs in a sensible portion. That's the [plate method](/plate-method-for-weight-loss/), and it works in restaurants too.

### 8. Choose the cooking method wisely

Grilled, baked, steamed and roasted dishes are usually lighter than fried, breaded or creamy ones.

### 9. Ask for sauces and dressings on the side

That way, you decide how much you use. Dip your fork in the dressing instead of pouring it over your salad.

### 10. Swap the side

Ask for vegetables or a salad instead of fries. Most restaurants are happy to do it.

## Portions and treats

### 11. Eat slowly and stop when you're satisfied

Restaurant portions are often huge. Eat slowly, enjoy the conversation and take the rest home. Our tips on [mindful eating](/mindful-eating-tips/) can help.

### 12. Share dessert

If you want dessert, enjoy it. Sharing it with someone gives you the taste for half the calories.

## Smart choices at popular restaurants

| Restaurant | Lighter choices |
|---|---|
| Italian | Tomato-based pasta (small portion), grilled fish, minestrone, thin-crust pizza with vegetables, shared |
| Burgers | Single patty, extra salad, side salad instead of fries, no extra cheese and bacon |
| Mexican | Fajitas, burrito bowl without the tortilla, beans, salsa and guacamole instead of sour cream and cheese |
| Asian | Stir-fries with lots of vegetables, sushi and sashimi, broth-based soups, steamed instead of fried dishes |
| Steakhouse | Lean steak or chicken with vegetables or a baked potato |

## What about alcohol?

Alcohol adds calories and can make it harder to stop eating. If you drink, choose one drink, alternate with water and skip sugary cocktails.

## The bottom line

One restaurant meal won't ruin your progress, and with a little planning it can fit easily into your week. Check the menu ahead, choose protein and vegetables, watch the extras and enjoy the company.
