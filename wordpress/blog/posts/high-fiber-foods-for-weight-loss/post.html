If protein is the first nutrient to think about for weight loss, fiber is the second. It keeps you full, slows down digestion and helps you eat less without feeling hungry. Yet most people eat far less fiber than they need.

Here's why fiber matters, how much you need and 20 easy foods to eat more of.

Why fiber helps with weight loss

  • It fills you up. High-fiber foods take up space in your stomach and take longer to digest.
  • Fewer calories per bite. Most high-fiber foods, like vegetables, fruit and beans, are low in calories for their volume.
  • More stable energy. Fiber slows down how quickly sugar enters your blood, which can help prevent energy crashes and cravings.
  • It's good for your gut. Fiber feeds the healthy bacteria in your gut and supports regular digestion.

How much fiber do you need?

A common recommendation for adults is about 25 to 38 grams of fiber per day. Many people get only half of that.

Increase fiber slowly, over one to two weeks, and drink enough water. Adding a lot of fiber at once can cause bloating.

Easy ways to eat more fiber

At breakfast

  • Choose oats or whole-grain bread instead of white bread or sugary cereal.
  • Add berries, a sliced pear or a spoon of chia seeds to your yogurt.

At lunch and dinner

  • Fill half your plate with vegetables, the core of the plate method.
  • Add a handful of beans or lentils to salads, soups and sauces.
  • Swap white rice and pasta for brown rice, quinoa or whole-wheat pasta, at least sometimes.
  • Keep the skin on potatoes and sweet potatoes.

As snacks

  • Fruit with a few nuts, veggie sticks with hummus or air-popped popcorn. More ideas in our list of healthy snacks.

A high-fiber day might look like this

  • Breakfast: overnight oats with chia seeds and raspberries (about 15 g)
  • Lunch: lentil salad with vegetables and feta (about 12 g)
  • Snack: an apple and a handful of almonds (about 7 g)
  • Dinner: salmon, broccoli and sweet potato with skin (about 9 g)

That's over 40 grams, without any supplements.

The bottom line

Fiber is one of the easiest ways to feel full while eating less. Add vegetables, fruit, beans and whole grains to your meals step by step, drink enough water and give your body a week or two to adjust.
