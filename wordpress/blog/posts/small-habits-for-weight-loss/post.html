Big changes are exciting for a week. Small habits last for years. Instead of a strict diet, try adding small, realistic habits one by one. Each one might seem tiny, but together they make a real difference.

Here are 21 small habits to choose from, and how to make them stick.

Eating habits

  1. Eat protein at breakfast. It keeps you full until lunch. Try our high-protein breakfast ideas.
  2. Fill half your plate with vegetables. The easiest way to eat more food with fewer calories. See the plate method.
  3. Use a smaller plate. A full small plate feels more satisfying than a half-empty big one.
  4. Eat slowly. Put your fork down between bites and give your body time to feel full.
  5. Eat at the table, without screens. You'll notice what and how much you eat. More in our guide to mindful eating.
  6. Never eat straight from the package. Put a portion in a bowl and put the rest away.
  7. Plan your snacks. Choose something with protein or fiber. Here are 15 healthy snacks.

Drinking habits

  1. Drink a glass of water when you wake up.
  2. Drink a glass of water before every meal. More tips in water and weight loss.
  3. Swap one sugary drink a day for water, sparkling water or unsweetened tea.
  4. Make your coffee simpler. Less syrup, less sugar, a splash of milk.

Movement habits

  1. Walk for 10 minutes after meals.
  2. Take the stairs whenever you can.
  3. Stand up and move every hour if you sit a lot.
  4. Do a short strength workout 2 to 3 times a week. Our 20-minute home workout needs no equipment.
  5. Track your steps and try to add a few hundred more each week.

Everyday habits

  1. Plan your meals for the week and shop with a list. Our meal prep guide shows you how.
  2. Keep healthy food visible and treats out of sight.
  3. Close the kitchen after dinner. Brushing your teeth helps. More in how to stop late-night snacking.
  4. Go to bed 30 minutes earlier. Better sleep means fewer cravings. See sleep and weight loss.
  5. Check in once a week. Weigh yourself or measure your waist on the same day each week and look at the trend, not single days.

How to make habits stick

Start with one or two

Don't try all 21 at once. Choose one or two habits that feel easy, and practice them until they're automatic, usually a few weeks. Then add the next one.

Make them tiny

"Walk for 10 minutes" is easier to start than "walk for an hour". Once the habit is there, you can always do more.

Link them to something you already do

New habits stick best when they follow an old one: after I make coffee, I drink a glass of water. After lunch, I walk for 10 minutes.

Make it easy

Put your walking shoes by the door. Keep a water bottle on your desk. Wash and cut vegetables when you come home from shopping.

Track your progress

A simple checklist on your fridge or in your phone, with a tick for every day you did your habit, is surprisingly motivating.

Don't aim for perfect

Missing one day doesn't matter. Try not to miss two in a row. Consistency over months is what counts.

The bottom line

Weight loss doesn't have to mean a complete life change overnight. Pick two habits from this list today, practice them until they feel normal and then add the next ones. Small steps, taken every day, get you there.
