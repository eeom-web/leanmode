If you're hungry again an hour after breakfast, your breakfast is probably missing one thing: protein. A breakfast built around protein keeps you full for longer, makes it easier to skip the mid-morning snack and helps you hold on to muscle while you lose weight.

The best part: a high-protein breakfast doesn't have to be complicated. Here are 12 ideas that take about 10 minutes, use simple ingredients and give you roughly 20 to 30 grams of protein.

How much protein should breakfast have?

For most adults, about 20 to 30 grams of protein per meal is a good target. That's roughly:

  • 3 eggs
  • a 200 g (7 oz) tub of Greek yogurt or skyr
  • 150 g (2/3 cup) of cottage cheese
  • one scoop of protein powder

Add some fiber (fruit, vegetables, oats or whole-grain bread) and you have a breakfast that keeps you full until lunch.

12 high-protein breakfast ideas

1. Greek yogurt bowl

Mix 200 g of Greek yogurt or skyr with a handful of berries and a tablespoon of nuts or seeds. Add a little cinnamon if you like it sweeter without sugar. About 20 g protein.

2. Veggie scrambled eggs

Scramble 3 eggs with spinach, tomatoes and peppers in one pan. Serve with one slice of whole-grain toast. About 22 g protein.

3. Overnight protein oats

The night before, mix 40 g of oats with 150 g of Greek yogurt and a splash of milk. In the morning, top with fruit. Zero minutes in the morning. About 20 g protein.

4. Cottage cheese toast

Spread cottage cheese on whole-grain toast and top it with sliced tomatoes, cucumber, salt and pepper. Or go sweet with berries and a drizzle of honey. About 20 g protein.

5. Egg muffins

Whisk 8 eggs with chopped vegetables and a little cheese, pour into a muffin tin and bake for 20 minutes. Keep them in the fridge for up to 4 days. Three muffins have about 20 g protein.

6. Protein smoothie

Blend one scoop of protein powder with a frozen banana, a handful of spinach and milk or water. Perfect when you eat on the go. About 25 g protein.

7. Smoked salmon wrap

Spread a whole-wheat tortilla with cream cheese, add smoked salmon, cucumber and a few salad leaves, and roll it up. About 22 g protein.

8. Breakfast burrito

Scrambled eggs, black beans and salsa in a whole-wheat tortilla. Make a few at once and freeze them for busy mornings. About 25 g protein.

9. Skyr with granola and fruit

Skyr is very high in protein and low in fat. Top it with a small handful of granola and sliced fruit. Keep the granola to about 2 tablespoons, it adds up fast. About 20 g protein.

10. Tofu scramble

Crumble firm tofu into a hot pan, season with turmeric, salt and pepper and add vegetables. A great plant-based option. About 20 g protein.

11. Turkey and egg breakfast plate

Two eggs, two slices of turkey breast, a handful of cherry tomatoes and a piece of fruit. No cooking skills needed. About 25 g protein.

12. Peanut butter protein toast

Whole-grain toast with peanut butter, banana slices and a glass of milk or a protein shake on the side. About 20 g protein.

Tips to make it easy every day

  • Prep ahead. Egg muffins, overnight oats and breakfast burritos can all be made on the weekend.
  • Keep the basics at home. Eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, oats and frozen berries cover most of the ideas above.
  • Use the plate method. Protein plus fruit or vegetables plus a small portion of carbs works for breakfast too. Read more in our guide to the plate method for weight loss.
  • Watch the extras. Granola, nut butter, juice and sweet coffee drinks can turn a light breakfast into a heavy one.

Not hungry in the morning? That's okay. You don't have to eat right after waking up. Just make your first meal of the day the high-protein one.

The bottom line

A high-protein breakfast is one of the easiest changes you can make when you want to lose weight. Pick two or three ideas from this list, keep the ingredients at home and rotate them through the week.
