You don't have to give up your favorite meals to lose weight. Often, small swaps are enough: a different drink, a different sauce, a different snack. Each one saves a few calories, and together they add up to a lot, without making you hungry.

Here are 15 simple swaps for every part of your day.

Breakfast swaps

1. Sugary cereal → oats with fruit

Many cereals are mostly sugar and don't keep you full for long. Oats with berries and Greek yogurt give you fiber and protein.

2. Flavored yogurt → plain Greek yogurt with berries

Flavored yogurts can contain a lot of added sugar. Plain Greek yogurt or skyr with fresh or frozen berries has more protein and less sugar.

3. Pastry → whole-grain toast with eggs

A croissant or muffin is gone in two minutes. Toast with eggs keeps you full until lunch. More ideas in our high-protein breakfast list.

Lunch and dinner swaps

4. Creamy sauces → tomato or yogurt-based sauces

Cream and cheese sauces are calorie-dense. Tomato sauce, salsa or a sauce made with Greek yogurt, lemon and herbs taste just as good.

5. Half the pasta → pasta plus vegetables

Use half the usual amount of pasta and add zucchini, spinach, peppers or mushrooms. Same full plate, fewer calories, more fiber.

6. White rice → half rice, half vegetables

Mix your rice with cauliflower rice or vegetables. Or try brown rice or quinoa for more fiber.

7. Fried → baked, grilled or air-fried

Baking, grilling or air-frying uses much less oil than deep-frying. Oven fries and air-fried chicken are great swaps.

8. Mayo → mustard, hummus or yogurt

On sandwiches and in salads, mustard, hummus or a yogurt dressing saves a lot of calories compared to mayonnaise.

9. Big burger → burger with a side salad

Keep the burger, skip the large fries. Or choose a smaller portion of fries and add a side salad.

Snack swaps

10. Chips → air-popped popcorn

Three cups of plain popcorn have about the same calories as a small handful of chips, and they fill you up much more.

11. Cookies → fruit with a few nuts

An apple with a tablespoon of peanut butter or a pear with a few almonds is sweet, filling and full of fiber.

12. Candy → frozen grapes or dark chocolate

Frozen grapes taste like little sorbet bites. Or enjoy one or two squares of dark chocolate slowly.

More ideas in our list of healthy snacks for weight loss.

Drink swaps

13. Soda → sparkling water with lemon

A can of soda has about 140 calories and around 35 g of sugar. Sparkling water with lemon, lime or a few berries has none.

14. Fancy coffee drinks → coffee with a splash of milk

A large flavored latte with syrup and cream can have as many calories as a meal. A regular coffee with milk has a fraction of that.

15. Juice → whole fruit

A glass of orange juice has the sugar of several oranges but almost no fiber. Eat the orange instead and drink water. See our guide to low-calorie drinks.

How to make swaps stick

  • Start with three. Pick the three swaps that would make the biggest difference for you.
  • Swap, don't ban. If you love pasta, don't quit it. Just change the portion and add vegetables.
  • Keep the swaps at home. Buy the better options so they're there when you need them. Our grocery list helps.

The bottom line

Small swaps are one of the most realistic ways to lose weight. They don't require willpower every day, they just become your new normal. Start with one swap per meal and see how easy it can be.
