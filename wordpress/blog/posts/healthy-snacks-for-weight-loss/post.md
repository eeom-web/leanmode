Snacking isn't bad for weight loss. The problem is *what* we usually snack on: chips, cookies and granola bars that are gone in a minute and leave you hungry again soon after.

A good snack has **protein or fiber (ideally both)**, keeps you full until your next meal and fits into your day. Here are 15 ideas, most of them under 200 calories.

## What makes a snack filling?

- **Protein:** yogurt, cottage cheese, eggs, cheese, edamame, jerky.
- **Fiber:** fruit, vegetables, whole grains, beans, nuts and seeds.
- **Volume:** foods with lots of water and fiber, like vegetables and fruit, fill your stomach for few calories.

Snacks made mostly of sugar or white flour are the opposite: quick to eat, quick to digest and not very filling.

## 15 healthy snacks for weight loss

### Protein snacks

1. **Greek yogurt or skyr with berries.** About 150 g of yogurt plus a handful of berries.
2. **Cottage cheese with cucumber or pineapple.** Savory or sweet, both work.
3. **Two hard-boiled eggs.** Cook a batch on Sunday and keep them in the fridge.
4. **Edamame.** A cup of steamed edamame with a pinch of salt has about 17 g of protein.
5. **Turkey or chicken roll-ups.** Roll slices of turkey breast around cucumber sticks or a little cheese.
6. **A protein shake.** Handy after a workout or when you're on the go.
7. **Beef jerky.** Choose one with little sugar and keep the portion small.

### Fiber snacks

8. **An apple with a tablespoon of peanut butter.** A classic for a reason.
9. **Veggie sticks with hummus.** Carrots, peppers, cucumber or celery with 2 to 3 tablespoons of hummus.
10. **Air-popped popcorn.** Three cups of plain popcorn are filling and only about 100 calories.
11. **A pear or orange with a few almonds.** Fruit plus a little fat and protein lasts longer than fruit alone.
12. **Roasted chickpeas.** Crunchy, salty and full of fiber and protein.

### Quick mixes

13. **Cheese and whole-grain crackers.** One slice of cheese and a few crackers.
14. **A small handful of nuts.** About 30 g (a small handful). Nuts are healthy but calorie-dense, so portion them.
15. **Chia pudding.** Mix 2 tablespoons of chia seeds with 150 ml of milk, leave it overnight and top with fruit.

## Smart snacking rules

- **Snack when you're actually hungry**, not because you're bored, tired or stressed. Our article on [emotional eating](/emotional-eating/) explains how to tell the difference.
- **Portion it out.** Never eat straight from the bag. Put a portion in a bowl and put the rest away.
- **Plan your snacks.** If you know you get hungry at 4 p.m., pack something good so you don't end up at the vending machine.
- **Drink water first.** Thirst can feel like hunger. More on that in our guide to [water and weight loss](/drink-more-water-for-weight-loss/).
- **Watch the "healthy" traps.** Granola bars, smoothies, dried fruit and trail mix can be high in sugar and calories.

## Do you need to snack at all?

No. If three meals a day keep you full, you don't need snacks. But if you get very hungry between meals and then overeat at dinner, a planned snack can actually help you eat less overall.

## The bottom line

The best snacks combine protein or fiber with a sensible portion. Pick three or four favorites from this list, keep them at home or at work and you'll never have to rely on the vending machine again. For more swaps like these, read our [15 healthy food swaps](/healthy-food-swaps/).
