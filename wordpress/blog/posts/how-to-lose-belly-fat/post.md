Belly fat is the number one thing many people want to lose. It's also surrounded by more myths than almost any other topic. Here's the honest truth about what works, what doesn't and where to start.

## Can you target belly fat?

Unfortunately, no. You can't choose where your body loses fat. Doing hundreds of crunches strengthens your stomach muscles, but it doesn't burn the fat on top of them.

When you lose weight overall, you lose fat from your whole body, including your belly. Where you lose it first depends largely on your genes, your age and your hormones. For many people, the belly is one of the last places to change. That's normal.

## Why belly fat matters

Fat around the waist isn't just about looks. A large waist is linked to a higher risk of health problems like type 2 diabetes and heart disease. That's why measuring your waist can be more useful than looking at the scale.

**How to measure:** wrap a tape measure around your stomach at the level of your belly button, breathe out normally and read the number. Measure once a month, at the same time of day.

## 7 habits that really help

### 1. Eat in a moderate calorie deficit

To lose fat, you need to eat a bit less energy than you use. You don't need to starve, a small, steady deficit is easier to keep up. The [plate method](/plate-method-for-weight-loss/) helps you eat less without counting calories.

### 2. Eat more protein

Protein keeps you full and helps you keep your muscles while you lose fat. Include a source of protein at every meal. Start with our [high-protein breakfast ideas](/high-protein-breakfast-ideas/).

### 3. Eat more fiber

Vegetables, fruit, beans, lentils and whole grains fill you up for fewer calories. See our list of [high-fiber foods](/high-fiber-foods-for-weight-loss/).

### 4. Cut back on sugary drinks and alcohol

Soda, juice, sweet coffee drinks and alcohol add a lot of calories without filling you up. Swapping them is one of the easiest changes you can make.

### 5. Do strength training

Strength training helps you keep muscle and changes your body shape. Our [20-minute home workout](/beginner-home-workout/) is a great place to start.

### 6. Move every day

Daily movement like walking adds up over weeks and months. Aim for 7,000 to 10,000 steps a day. Here's a [beginner walking plan](/walking-for-weight-loss/).

### 7. Sleep and manage stress

Poor sleep and constant stress make cravings stronger and healthy habits harder. Read our tips on [sleep and weight loss](/sleep-and-weight-loss/).

## Myths you can ignore

- **"Crunches burn belly fat."** They strengthen your core, which is good, but they don't target fat.
- **"Detox teas flatten your stomach."** At best, they make you lose water. At worst, they're laxatives.
- **"Waist trainers slim your waist."** They squeeze your stomach while you wear them. Nothing more.
- **"Certain foods melt belly fat."** No food burns fat. Lemon water, apple cider vinegar and green tea are fine to enjoy, but they aren't magic.
- **"You need to cut out all carbs."** You can lose belly fat while eating potatoes, rice and bread in sensible portions.

## How long does it take?

It depends on how much you want to lose. A healthy, sustainable pace for most people is about **0.5 to 1 kg (1 to 2 lb) per week**. Belly fat often goes last, so be patient and track your waist measurement, not just the scale.

> If you have a lot of weight to lose or a health condition, your doctor can help you set a safe goal and check things like blood pressure and blood sugar.

## The bottom line

There's no shortcut and no special exercise for belly fat. What works is boring but effective: eat a bit less, eat more protein and fiber, move every day, do some strength training and sleep well. Do that consistently, and your waist will follow.
