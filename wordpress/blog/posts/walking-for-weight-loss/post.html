Walking is the most underrated way to lose weight. You don't need a gym, any equipment or a lot of fitness, and it's easy on your joints. Better still, it's something you can actually keep doing for years.

Here's why walking works, how much you need and a simple 4-week plan to get started.

Why walking works for weight loss

  • It burns calories without wearing you out. A brisk 30-minute walk burns roughly 100 to 200 calories, depending on your weight and speed. Done every day, that adds up.
  • It doesn't make you ravenous. Hard workouts can make some people very hungry. Walking usually doesn't.
  • It's easy to stick with. The best exercise for weight loss is the one you do consistently, and walking is easy to fit into almost any day.
  • It's good for much more than weight. Regular walking supports your heart, your mood, your sleep and your blood sugar.

Exercise works best together with what you eat. Walking won't undo a big portion of fries, but combined with balanced meals it makes a real difference.

How much should you walk?

A good long-term goal is 30 to 60 minutes of brisk walking on most days, or around 7,000 to 10,000 steps a day. "Brisk" means you can still talk, but you couldn't sing.

If you're starting from very few steps, don't jump straight to 10,000. Increase slowly, by about 1,000 steps a day each week.

Your 4-week walking plan

On the other days, rest or go for an easy stroll. If a week feels too hard, simply repeat it. There's no rush.

How to make walking more effective

Walk briskly

Speed matters more than distance. Swing your arms, take quicker steps and walk like you're slightly late for an appointment.

Add intervals

Once walking feels easy, try this: 2 minutes brisk, 1 minute as fast as you can, and repeat 5 to 8 times.

Use hills and stairs

Walking uphill or taking the stairs makes your legs work harder and raises your heart rate without running.

Walk after meals

A 10 to 15 minute walk after lunch or dinner is an easy habit to build, and it can help with digestion and blood sugar.

10 easy ways to get more steps every day

  1. Take a short walk after every meal.
  2. Walk while you're on the phone.
  3. Get off the bus one stop early.
  4. Park farther away from the entrance.
  5. Take the stairs instead of the elevator.
  6. Walk to the shops for small errands.
  7. Meet a friend for a walk instead of a coffee.
  8. Set a reminder to stand up and move every hour.
  9. Listen to a podcast or audiobook you only play while walking.
  10. Track your steps with your phone and try to beat yesterday.

What you need

Just comfortable shoes with good support and clothes for the weather. A water bottle helps on longer walks. That's it.

Have joint problems, heart problems or another health condition? Talk to your doctor before you start a new exercise routine.
