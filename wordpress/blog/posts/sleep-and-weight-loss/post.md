When people think about weight loss, they think about food and exercise. Sleep is the forgotten third piece. Yet a few bad nights can make everything else harder: you're hungrier, your cravings are stronger and you have less energy to move.

Here's why sleep matters and ten simple ways to sleep better.

## How sleep affects your weight

- **More hunger.** Too little sleep affects the hormones that control hunger and fullness. Many people feel hungrier after a short night.
- **Stronger cravings.** Tired brains want quick energy, which usually means sweet, salty and fatty foods.
- **More time to snack.** The longer you're awake in the evening, the more chances there are for [late-night snacking](/stop-late-night-snacking/).
- **Less energy to move.** When you're tired, the walk or workout is the first thing you skip.
- **Weaker willpower.** Decisions are harder when you're exhausted, including food decisions.

## How much sleep do you need?

Most adults need **7 to 9 hours of sleep per night**. If you regularly sleep less than 7 hours, improving your sleep may help your weight loss as much as changing what you eat.

## 10 tips to sleep better

### 1. Keep a regular schedule

Go to bed and get up at about the same time every day, even on weekends. Your body loves rhythm.

### 2. Create a wind-down routine

Spend the last 30 minutes before bed doing something calm: reading, stretching, a warm shower or listening to quiet music.

### 3. Put screens away

Phones, tablets and laptops keep your brain busy. Try to put them away 30 to 60 minutes before bed, or at least turn down the brightness.

### 4. Keep your bedroom cool, dark and quiet

A temperature around 16 to 19 °C (60 to 67 °F), blackout curtains and earplugs if needed make a big difference.

### 5. Watch your caffeine

Caffeine can stay in your body for many hours. Try to have your last coffee, black tea or energy drink before early afternoon.

### 6. Be careful with alcohol

A drink might help you fall asleep, but it often makes your sleep lighter and more restless in the second half of the night.

### 7. Don't go to bed too full or too hungry

Finish big meals about 2 to 3 hours before bed. If you're hungry, a small snack like yogurt or a banana is fine.

### 8. Get daylight in the morning

Go outside for 10 to 15 minutes in the morning. Daylight helps set your internal clock, so you feel sleepy at the right time in the evening.

### 9. Move during the day

Regular activity helps you sleep better. A daily walk is perfect. Our [walking plan for beginners](/walking-for-weight-loss/) is a great place to start.

### 10. Don't lie awake for hours

If you can't sleep after about 20 minutes, get up, do something calm in dim light and go back to bed when you feel sleepy.

> If you snore loudly, wake up gasping or feel exhausted even after a full night, talk to your doctor. Sleep problems like sleep apnea are common and treatable.

## The bottom line

Sleep isn't a luxury when you want to lose weight, it's part of the plan. Aim for 7 to 9 hours, keep a regular schedule and build a calm evening routine. Start tonight with one tip from this list.
