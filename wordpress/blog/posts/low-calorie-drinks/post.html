What you drink can make or break your weight loss. Drinks can contain a surprising number of calories, and because they don't fill you up, those calories come on top of what you eat.

Here's what to drink, what to limit and some easy swaps.

Why drinks matter so much

Your body doesn't register liquid calories the same way as food. A glass of juice or a sweet coffee doesn't make you eat less at your next meal. That's why cutting liquid calories is one of the easiest ways to lose weight without eating less.

The best drinks for weight loss

Water

The best choice: zero calories and essential for your body. Add lemon, lime, cucumber, mint or berries for flavor. Read our tips on drinking more water.

Sparkling water

Perfect if you miss the fizz of soda. Choose unsweetened versions.

Unsweetened tea

Green tea, black tea and herbal teas, hot or iced, have almost no calories.

Black coffee or coffee with a splash of milk

Plain coffee has almost no calories. It's the sugar, syrup and cream that add up.

Milk or unsweetened plant milk

Milk contains protein and can be part of a snack. Unsweetened soy milk is a good plant-based option with protein too.

Drinks to limit

Numbers vary by brand and recipe, but you get the idea: two or three of these a day can add up to the calories of a full meal.

Easy drink swaps

  • Soda → sparkling water with lemon or lime
  • Juice → a piece of whole fruit and a glass of water
  • Flavored latte → regular coffee with milk and cinnamon
  • Sweet iced tea → unsweetened iced tea with lemon and mint
  • Energy drink → black coffee or green tea
  • Cocktail → wine spritzer or a drink with sparkling water instead of soda

What about diet soda?

Diet sodas have almost no calories and can help some people move away from regular soda. They're fine in moderation, but water and unsweetened tea are still the better everyday choices.

And alcohol?

Alcohol has a lot of calories, can make you hungrier and lowers your guard around food. You don't have to quit, but it helps to set a limit: for example, one or two drinks on the weekend. Alternate each drink with a glass of water.

The bottom line

Water, sparkling water, unsweetened tea and plain coffee are your best friends. Be careful with juice, smoothies, sweet coffee drinks, energy drinks and alcohol. Swapping just one sugary drink a day can make a real difference over a few months.
